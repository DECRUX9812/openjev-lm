**Post:**
Most companies sit on years of data they have never used — old job postings, old enquiries, old support tickets. The reason was always cost: running AI over all of it means paying per call.

That is changing. I spent one night training a small model on a CPU-only machine — no cloud, no GPU — until it could read a job posting and make the same kind of call a paid hosted service makes: in about six milliseconds, for nothing.

It now matches human labels on 92.9% of a held-out set of postings; the paid model it learned from scores 97.1%. A lighter version agrees with the paid service on 99.39% of 2,631 postings.

When a decision costs nothing to make, the question flips: not "can I afford to look?" but "why not look at everything?"
